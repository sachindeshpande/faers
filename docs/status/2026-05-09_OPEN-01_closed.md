## Key Finding

The C.5.6.r warning is triggered by the **presence of the cross-reference element**,
not by the OID value carried inside it. Stripping OID `…2.1.2.3` entirely or swapping
it to `…2.1.2.1` produced identical warning behaviour.

This means:
- The warning **cannot be suppressed** for IND submissions that carry cross-referenced
  IND numbers
- CA+AE (not CA+AA) is the correct and expected ACK type for all such submissions
- The OID value used in the cross-reference element has no effect on acceptance or warning

## What was NOT achieved (and why it doesn't matter)

The original OPEN-01 goal was to confirm CA+AE **without** the C.5.6.r warning, which
would have allowed promotion of `crossReportedInd` to `proven_safe` on a warning-free
emission state. That goal is unachievable — the warning is structural to the CDER_IND
channel. However:

- All 7 submissions are **accepted** (CA+AE)
- The warning is **informational only** — it does not block processing
- `proven_safe` is the correct verdict because cross-references are accepted by FDA
- Production IND submissions with cross-referenced IND numbers should always expect
  CA+AE, never CA+AA
